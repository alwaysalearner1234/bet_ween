# Phase 0: Day 3

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Conduct interviews 1-4
- Interviewed 4 participants from hackathon community
- Key insights gathered:
  - Participants feel awkward approaching strangers without "permission"
  - Shared interests are the #1 factor in starting conversations
  - Fear of being perceived as "intrusive" is the main barrier
  - People want to know others are "open to meeting" before signaling themselves
  - Conversation starters about shared event context work well

### 2. Wireframe all 11 MVP screens in Figma
Created Figma frames for all 11 MVP screens:

1. **Welcome Screen** - Event/community selection
2. **Conversation Type** - Casual, Hobbies, Ideas, Fun, Deep, etc.
3. **Choose Interests** - Interest chip selection
4. **Vibe Preferences** - Optional: creative, chill, ambitious, funny, gaming, music, tech
5. **Open to Meeting** - ON/OFF toggle
6. **Compatible People** - Limited set, no exact location
7. **Mutual Connection** - Opt-in match interface
8. **One Conversation Bridge** - Single prompt, then quiet
9. **Post-Conversation** - Continue / Not this time
10. **Chat Screen** - Only after mutual continuation
11. **Block/Report** - Safety controls
